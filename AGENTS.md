# Mars Ops project instructions

Before any project change, read `docs/STATE.md` and `docs/SOURCES.md`. Before changing the EVA helmet, terrain, locomotion, or XR setup, also read `docs/VR_INTEGRATION.md` and `docs/VR_TEST.md`.

Preserve the existing Mission Control, Ship Console, route planner, Supabase adapter, and pinned `package-lock.json`. Keep DTM-derived geometry separate from photographs. Suit/vital readouts are demonstration values, not real telemetry or medical measurements. Route calculation is not a safety certification. Do not claim NASA endorsement, live sensor data, or Quest-device verification; pending physical checks must say `NOT TESTED ON DEVICE`. Never bypass certificate validation or headset safety boundaries.
